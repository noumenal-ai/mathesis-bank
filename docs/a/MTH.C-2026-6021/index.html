The two-clique quantum junction tree theorem (Lauritzen–Zwiernik, arXiv:2605.19453, Theorem 3.1): over the acyclic two-clique base, the base's compatibility datum decides base-side reconstruction. For strictly positive consistent marginals, a quantum Markov completion exists iff Tr T(R) = 1; the completion is then unique and equals the normalized logarithmic candidate σ(R) = T(R)/Tr T(R). Together with trC_le_one (the trace bound) this is the full theorem.
